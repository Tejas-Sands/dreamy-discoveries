# Ozzy and the Paper Crown

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Professor Ozzy

**Featured cast:** Professor Ozzy, Taffy

**Setting:** castle

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** A caring leader apologizes for bossing friends and gives their ideas a real turn.

Professor Ozzy keeps every castle choice for himself, even during Taffy’s supposed turn with the crown. He apologizes, follows her garden plan as map keeper, and makes space for both leaders.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Play a make-believe castle adventure with Taffy and a paper crown.
- **First attempt:** Order Taffy to stay by the gate while he makes every adventure choice.
- **Different second attempt:** Offer Taffy a pretend leadership turn while still telling her exactly what to do.
- **Consequence:** Taffy stops playing because her own castle ideas have no place.
- **Repair:** Apologize specifically, listen to Taffy’s garden plan, and become the map keeper while Taffy leads.
- **Ending:** They exchange the crown for full turns and combine a garden invitation with a book room.
