## Story beats

- **Goal:** Collect six smooth stones for Ben to make a story picture path.
- **First attempt:** Stop collecting to study interesting patterns on one stone.
- **Different second attempt:** Offer a spoken story and hope Ben no longer needs the promised stones.
- **Consequence:** Ben waits with a half-built path and cannot move the story picture onward.
- **Repair:** Explain the unfinished promise, collect the remaining stones using a count card, and bring them to Ben.
- **Ending:** Ben completes the picture path and both friends tell the whole journey together.
