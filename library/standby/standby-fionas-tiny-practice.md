# Fiona's Tiny Practice

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Fiona

**Featured cast:** Fiona, Professor Ozzy

**Setting:** circus

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Practicing one small step patiently helps us learn a new skill.

Fiona wants a soft scarf trick for the meadow circus, but a rushed spin and a bigger toss keep spoiling her catch. She practices a tiny toss from the ground, adds one step at a time, and performs the small trick she learned herself.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Learn a low, soft-scarf toss-and-catch trick for a pretend circus.
- **First attempt:** Rush a toss and spin before watching the scarf.
- **Different second attempt:** Throw much higher to force extra catching time.
- **Consequence:** The scarf keeps landing on the mat, and Fiona feels ready to quit.
- **Repair:** Practice one low toss without spinning, watch it float, then add a small step.
- **Ending:** Fiona performs her gentle trick and begins learning the next tiny step patiently.
