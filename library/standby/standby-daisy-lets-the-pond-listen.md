# Daisy Lets the Pond Listen

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Daisy

**Featured cast:** Daisy, Fiona

**Setting:** pond

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Leaving quiet pauses helps friends hear each other and make music together.

Daisy wants to sing a pond song with Fiona, but loud singing and early answers hide Fiona’s rhythm. Daisy listens to the whole pattern and gives each friend a clear musical turn.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Sing a shared pond song with Fiona.
- **First attempt:** Sing louder over Fiona’s tapping to pull the rhythm together.
- **Different second attempt:** Guess Fiona’s next beat and sing before her tapping finishes.
- **Consequence:** Fiona puts her tapping stick down because her part cannot be heard.
- **Repair:** Hear Fiona’s whole rhythm, agree on a nod, and leave two quiet beats.
- **Ending:** Daisy waits for Fiona’s complete tapping answer before singing the next verse.
