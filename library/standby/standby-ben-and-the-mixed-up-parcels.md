# Ben and the Mixed-Up Parcels

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Ben

**Featured cast:** Ben, Fiona

**Setting:** city

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** When your labels cause a mix-up, saying exactly what happened helps you put things right.

Ben and Fiona pack their own ribbons and crayons for a city craft afternoon, but Ben swaps the labels. Closing the wrong box and then silently swapping the boxes hide the confusion until he admits the mistake and checks each label with Fiona.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Pack two craft parcels correctly for an afternoon with Fiona.
- **First attempt:** Close the ribbon box after noticing it has the crayon label.
- **Different second attempt:** Swap the closed boxes around without checking their contents or explaining.
- **Consequence:** Fiona cannot find her ribbons and Ben cannot start his drawing.
- **Repair:** Explain the label swap, open both parcels, and label the verified contents together.
- **Ending:** Both use the correctly marked parcels for their original craft afternoon.
