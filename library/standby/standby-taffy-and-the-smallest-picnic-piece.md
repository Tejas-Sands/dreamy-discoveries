# Taffy and the Smallest Picnic Piece

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Taffy

**Featured cast:** Taffy, Ben

**Setting:** meadow

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Sharing fairly means offering a friend a real part of what you value.

Taffy saves the last carrot biscuit for her perfect picnic with Ben. When a crumb and a pretend turn leave Ben outside the treat, she chooses to divide the valued biscuit fairly.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Enjoy the last carrot biscuit at a picnic with Ben.
- **First attempt:** Offer Ben only crumbs while keeping the whole biscuit.
- **Different second attempt:** Offer Ben a pretend tasting turn without sharing food.
- **Consequence:** Ben puts away his empty plate and the picnic becomes quiet.
- **Repair:** Break the last biscuit along its middle and offer Ben first choice.
- **Ending:** Both friends enjoy their biscuit pieces and their picnic together.
