# Ben's Thank-You Patch

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Ben

**Featured cast:** Ben, Grandpa Tilly

**Setting:** garden

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Gratitude grows when you notice a friend’s quiet help and answer it with care.

Ben proudly plants a garden row while Tilly quietly steadies pots and saves seeds from a breeze. Ben praises only himself and then offers a vague thanks without noticing Tilly’s remaining cleanup, before naming the help and preparing a thoughtful place for Tilly to plant.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Plant a tidy garden row with Tilly.
- **First attempt:** Celebrate his planting while ignoring Tilly’s steadying and sorting work.
- **Different second attempt:** Call a quick general thanks while leaving Tilly the entire cleanup.
- **Consequence:** Tilly has no time to plant his own seeds and their shared afternoon feels one-sided.
- **Repair:** Thank Tilly for specific help, take over cleanup, and prepare a comfortable planting spot.
- **Ending:** Both finish their own planting rows and label Tilly’s row the thank-you patch.
