# Ben Measures a Little

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Ben

**Featured cast:** Ben, Daisy

**Setting:** kitchen

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Listening to every step helps you choose the right amount for a shared job.

Ben wants smiling faces on already baked cookies, but he interrupts Daisy and floods one with icing. Guessing the next step sends sprinkles sliding too, until he listens, measures a small spoonful, and gives each smile time to settle.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Decorate already baked, cooled cookies with smiling faces for Daisy.
- **First attempt:** Interrupt Daisy’s explanation and pour too much ready-made icing.
- **Different second attempt:** Guess that lots of sprinkles will fix the wet icing.
- **Consequence:** Cookie faces disappear under sliding decorations and Daisy’s pattern is lost.
- **Repair:** Listen to all three steps, ask about the spoon, and measure a little icing before decorating.
- **Ending:** Their finished smiling cookies sit together on the shared tray.
