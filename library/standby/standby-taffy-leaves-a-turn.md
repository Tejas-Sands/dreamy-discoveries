# Taffy Leaves a Turn

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Taffy

**Featured cast:** Taffy, Fiona

**Setting:** autumn

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Waiting for the agreed signal gives a friend a whole turn to enjoy.

Taffy and Fiona make leaf prints with one stamping pad, but Taffy keeps reaching before Fiona finishes. After crowding a print and then counting an unfairly short turn, Taffy agrees to wait until Fiona passes the leaf token.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Make a leaf-print trail together with Fiona.
- **First attempt:** Grab the stamp pad while Fiona is still printing.
- **Different second attempt:** Count a short turn aloud and take the pad before Fiona finishes.
- **Consequence:** Fiona has a smudged print and stops enjoying the game.
- **Repair:** Agree that passing the leaf token signals a finished turn, then wait for it.
- **Ending:** Both complete clear prints that join into one autumn trail.
