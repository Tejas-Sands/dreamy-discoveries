# Daisy's Slow Parade

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Daisy

**Featured cast:** Daisy, Grandpa Tilly

**Setting:** park

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Including a friend means making a useful place and a pace they can share.

Daisy’s quick park parade leaves Grandpa Tilly’s handmade ribbon behind, and asking him to watch does not give him a real part. Daisy makes him the ribbon guide and follows the steady pace he chooses.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** March a ribbon parade with Grandpa Tilly.
- **First attempt:** Call Tilly to join while marching away too quickly.
- **Different second attempt:** Ask Tilly to sit at the finish and wave instead of marching.
- **Consequence:** The ribbon drags, and Tilly has no marching part in the parade he helped prepare.
- **Repair:** Invite Tilly to guide the ribbon, ask about a comfortable pace, and march beside him.
- **Ending:** The friends complete the loop together with Tilly leading the shared stop signal.
