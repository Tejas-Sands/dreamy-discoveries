# Taffy's Crooked Sandcastle

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Taffy

**Featured cast:** Taffy, Daisy

**Setting:** beach

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** When one way keeps failing, perseverance means noticing the problem and changing a useful step.

Taffy wants a pretend sandcastle with a wide gate between low walls, but dry sand and a tall skinny tower both tumble. She tests gently damp sand and makes a broad base, giving their crooked castle a gate that stays.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Build a pretend sandcastle with a gate between low walls for Daisy.
- **First attempt:** Pile loose dry sand into a tall wall.
- **Different second attempt:** Stack a skinny bucket tower higher to make room.
- **Consequence:** The doorway keeps collapsing and Daisy cannot use it.
- **Repair:** Try gently damp sand and spread a broad base before shaping the doorway.
- **Ending:** Daisy uses the doorway and both enjoy their sturdy crooked castle.
