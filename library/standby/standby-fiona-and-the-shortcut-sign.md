# Fiona and the Shortcut Sign

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Fiona

**Featured cast:** Fiona, Grandpa Tilly

**Setting:** forest

**Moral:** Telling the truth about a mistake helps us repair the things friends depend on.

Fiona moves a picnic arrow to claim a shortcut and then hides the loose marker when it points the wrong way. She tells Grandpa Tilly what happened and restores the arrows along their familiar forest path.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Finish clear picnic trail markers on a familiar short forest loop.
- **First attempt:** Move a turn arrow to make a shorter route through the picnic clearing.
- **Different second attempt:** Hide the loose arrow beneath the picnic cloth instead of admitting the change.
- **Consequence:** The picnic arrows no longer agree, so Grandpa Tilly pauses the marker check.
- **Repair:** Explain exactly which arrow she moved, retie it at the proper turn, and test every marker.
- **Ending:** Fiona notices a tilted finish marker and openly asks Tilly to steady it while she reties it.
