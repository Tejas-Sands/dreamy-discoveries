# Fiona Chooses a Fair Finish

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Fiona

**Featured cast:** Fiona, Taffy

**Setting:** playground

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Keeping shared game rules matters more than making yourself the winner.

Fiona wants to win a beanbag game with Taffy, so she takes an extra throw and moves the throwing line. When Taffy stops enjoying the game, Fiona restores the agreed rules, resets her score, and celebrates Taffy’s fair finish.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Play a friendly three-throw beanbag game with Taffy.
- **First attempt:** Count an extra practice throw as a scoring throw after missing.
- **Different second attempt:** Move the shared throwing line closer only for her own turn.
- **Consequence:** Taffy puts down her beanbag because the game no longer uses their agreement.
- **Repair:** Restore the throwing line, remove the extra score, and restart with three alternating throws each.
- **Ending:** Taffy wins the fair round and Fiona happily celebrates with her.
