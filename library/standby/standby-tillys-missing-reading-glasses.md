# Tilly's Missing Reading Glasses

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Grandpa Tilly

**Featured cast:** Grandpa Tilly, Taffy

**Setting:** bedroom

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Returning books and reading things to clear homes helps everyone enjoy story time.

Grandpa Tilly clears a reading seat by hiding shared books and his own glasses, then rushing more things into a box. When story time stalls, he makes visible homes that help both friends find what they need.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Read a shared picture book with Taffy using Tilly's own reading glasses.
- **First attempt:** Hide shared books and his own glasses inside a cushion pile to clear a seat.
- **Different second attempt:** Rush remaining objects into an unmarked box without sorting.
- **Consequence:** Taffy cannot find Tilly's reading glasses or their chosen picture book, so reading stops.
- **Repair:** Open both hiding places, sort each item, and label a glasses tray and book shelf with pictures.
- **Ending:** Taffy finds Tilly's glasses easily for their next book and helps return them to the tray.
