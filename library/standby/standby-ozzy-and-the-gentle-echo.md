# Ozzy and the Gentle Echo

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Professor Ozzy

**Featured cast:** Professor Ozzy, Daisy

**Setting:** mountain

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** When a playful imitation hurts a friend, stop, apologize, and choose words that welcome their voice.

Professor Ozzy teases Daisy’s practice note, then tries turning his imitation into a game she must join. He stops, apologizes, and rebuilds their duet around words Daisy wants to share.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Make a gentle mountain echo song with Daisy in a flat meadow below the hills.
- **First attempt:** Imitate Daisy’s wobbly practice note as a teasing joke.
- **Different second attempt:** Turn his teasing imitation into a game and ask Daisy to repeat it with him.
- **Consequence:** Daisy becomes quiet and their two-voice echo song stops.
- **Repair:** Stop the imitation, apologize for mocking her note, ask what would help, and echo the words Daisy chooses respectfully.
- **Ending:** Their finished song repeats each friend’s chosen words and welcomes a wobbly note too.
