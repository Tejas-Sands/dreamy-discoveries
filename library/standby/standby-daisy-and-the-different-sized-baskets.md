# Daisy and the Different-Sized Baskets

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Daisy

**Featured cast:** Daisy, Professor Ozzy

**Setting:** farm

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** A fair shared job gives each friend useful work that fits their abilities.

Daisy insists that she and Professor Ozzy carry identical baskets to set a farm picnic, but the loads do not fit their different ways of moving. She gives each friend a well-fitting basket and divides the real work so both can help.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Carry picnic cloths and cups to the farm table with Professor Ozzy.
- **First attempt:** Give both friends equally large baskets despite different carrying abilities.
- **Different second attempt:** Give both tiny baskets and pack the same few cups, leaving the cloths behind.
- **Consequence:** The table lacks cloths and cups, and Ozzy feels his useful help is being overlooked.
- **Repair:** Ask what each can carry, fit baskets to abilities, and split cloths and cups into practical loads.
- **Ending:** Both friends finish carrying and spread the tablecloth they delivered together.
