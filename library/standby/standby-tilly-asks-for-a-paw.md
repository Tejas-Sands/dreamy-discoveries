# Tilly Asks for a Paw

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Grandpa Tilly

**Moral:** Asking for one specific kind of help lets friends build something together.

Grandpa Tilly wants to build a pretend desert shelter, but his two solo methods keep pulling it apart. He names the help he needs and builds alongside Professor Ozzy.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Build a small pretend shade shelter for a desert story picnic.
- **First attempt:** Balance the cloth on two short cardboard posts alone.
- **Different second attempt:** Clamp the cloth beneath his shell while trying to tie both corners.
- **Consequence:** The cloth pulls the posts sideways and Ozzy cannot set out the story cards.
- **Repair:** Ask Ozzy to hold one cloth corner while Tilly ties a loose loop, then exchange roles.
- **Ending:** Both friends read their desert story beneath the steady pretend shelter.
