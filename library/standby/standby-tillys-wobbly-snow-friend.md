# Tilly's Wobbly Snow Friend

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Grandpa Tilly

**Featured cast:** Grandpa Tilly, Daisy

**Setting:** snow

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Combining useful ideas and testing them together can make a shared project stronger.

Grandpa Tilly and Daisy each try a sensible snow-building idea, but neither whole design stays together. Tilly suggests combining Daisy’s broad base with his firm snow joints and testing each layer together.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Build a snow friend that can wear their little paper scarf.
- **First attempt:** Stack tall narrow snowballs alone without a broad base.
- **Different second attempt:** Start a separate flat snow mound that supports a head but lacks packed joints.
- **Consequence:** Neither snow shape stays together long enough to hold their scarf.
- **Repair:** Combine Daisy’s broad base and Tilly’s carefully packed joints, checking each layer together.
- **Ending:** Their stable snow friend wears the scarf and they leave a shared paw-and-wing signature.
