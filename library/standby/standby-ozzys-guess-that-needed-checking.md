# Ozzy's Guess That Needed Checking

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Professor Ozzy

**Featured cast:** Professor Ozzy, Ben

**Setting:** jungle

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Check what happened before blaming a friend, and repair a mistake when you discover it.

Professor Ozzy assumes Ben scattered their leaf puzzle and asks him to fix it without checking. A careful repeat of Ozzy’s own page turn reveals the draft, and he apologizes and rebuilds alongside Ben.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Complete a leaf puzzle with Ben on a jungle picnic mat.
- **First attempt:** Assume Ben scattered the leaves because he was standing nearby.
- **Different second attempt:** Tell Ben to sort the fallen leaves before checking their original places.
- **Consequence:** Ben feels unheard and the puzzle cannot be restored from Ozzy’s untested guess.
- **Repair:** Repeat his own page turn, discover his wing made the draft, apologize, and rebuild using the picture guide.
- **Ending:** They hold pages down and check the picture together before moving any puzzle piece.
