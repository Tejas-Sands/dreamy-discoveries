# Daisy's Cloud Picture

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Daisy

**Featured cast:** Daisy, Taffy

**Setting:** sky

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Friends can see different things in the same cloud and respect both ideas.

Daisy sees a duck in a cloud while Taffy sees a carrot, and Daisy’s insistence spoils their shared drawing. Daisy listens to Taffy’s view and makes room for both pictures on the same page.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Draw a shared cloud picture with Taffy while sitting on the ground.
- **First attempt:** Insist Taffy call the cloud a duck and copy Daisy’s drawing.
- **Different second attempt:** Replace Taffy’s carrot marks with duck details to make the page agree.
- **Consequence:** Taffy stops drawing because her idea has no place on their shared paper.
- **Repair:** Listen to Taffy’s view, restore her carrot drawing, and leave two sides for different ideas.
- **Ending:** They spot another cloud and add two different pictures without arguing.
