# Fiona and the Last Decoration

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Fiona

**Featured cast:** Fiona, Ben

**Setting:** candy

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Sharing something you value can show a friend that their happiness matters too.

Fiona saves the last shiny paper star for her own pretend party hat and offers Ben a scrap that will not decorate his. She chooses to share the star she loves, helps place it on Ben’s hat, and enjoys their party together.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Finish two hats for a pretend candy-shop party with Ben.
- **First attempt:** Keep the last shiny paper star on her own hat despite having two already.
- **Different second attempt:** Offer Ben an unwanted scrap and then only a brief turn looking at the star.
- **Consequence:** Ben’s hat stays unfinished while Fiona’s treasured star waits apart from their shared party.
- **Repair:** Remove the extra shiny star from her hat, choose to give it to Ben, and help attach it.
- **Ending:** Fiona and Ben admire both finished hats and begin their pretend party together.
