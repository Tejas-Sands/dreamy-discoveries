# Ozzy's One-Star-at-a-Time Map

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Professor Ozzy

**Featured cast:** Professor Ozzy, Fiona

**Setting:** space

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Quiet courage can begin with one small step and grow as you try the next.

Professor Ozzy feels unsure while drawing a pretend moon-camp map and tries rushing the whole trail, then copying Fiona’s. He finds a quiet way to begin by choosing one paper star and building his own story path one step at a time.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Draw a pretend moon-camp story map with a trail of paper stars.
- **First attempt:** Draw the whole star trail at once and stop when the many choices feel overwhelming.
- **Different second attempt:** Copy Fiona’s quick trail without choosing his own steps, so the path misses his story places.
- **Consequence:** His map remains unfinished and he feels unable to begin his own story.
- **Repair:** Choose and place one star near the paper tent, then add and check one reachable step at a time.
- **Ending:** His finished map links the pretend tent, paper moon garden, and story mat, and he narrates its journey.
