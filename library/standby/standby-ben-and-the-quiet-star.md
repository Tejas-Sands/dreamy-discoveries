# Ben and the Quiet Star

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Ben

**Featured cast:** Ben, Professor Ozzy

**Setting:** night

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Asking for one specific kind of help lets you keep learning while a friend supports you.

Ben wants to finish a star map for Ozzy, but pretending to know and then guessing alone leave one dot misplaced. He finally asks Ozzy to help find the starting star while keeping the drawing for himself.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Draw a simple star map to enjoy with Ozzy.
- **First attempt:** Pretend to understand the starting point and draw from the wrong dot.
- **Different second attempt:** Keep guessing alone while saying that no help is needed.
- **Consequence:** His lines cross and he cannot find the quiet little star in his picture.
- **Repair:** Ask Ozzy to help identify the starting star, then draw the next dots himself.
- **Ending:** Both use Ben’s finished map to find the stars they were watching.
