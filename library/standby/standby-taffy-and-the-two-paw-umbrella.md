# Taffy and the Two-Paw Umbrella

**Status:** Available

**Hero:** Taffy

**Featured cast:** Taffy, Grandpa Tilly

**Setting:** rainy

**Target length:** 5.5 minutes (final length follows voice timing)

**Moral:** Sharing shelter works when you listen and make room at your friend’s pace.

Taffy wants to reach a covered reading bench with Grandpa Tilly under one umbrella. Walking ahead and then urging him to hurry leave the shelter uneven, so she listens, centers the umbrella, and matches their steps.

## Story beats

- **Goal:** Reach the covered reading bench with Tilly while sharing one umbrella.
- **First attempt:** Walk ahead while calling that Tilly can follow.
- **Different second attempt:** Ask Tilly to hurry with hopping steps while tilting the umbrella toward herself.
- **Consequence:** Tilly pauses with a damp shoulder and the walk no longer feels shared.
- **Repair:** Ask about Tilly’s pace, center the canopy, and walk beside him.
- **Ending:** Both reach the dry reading bench and enjoy the book they carried.
